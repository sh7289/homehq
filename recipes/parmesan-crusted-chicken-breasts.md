For 2 large chicken breasts: slice horizontally into 4 cutlets; season with 1/2 tsp kosher salt + 1/4 tsp pepper. Whisk 1 egg + 1 tbsp Dijon. Mix 1/2 cup grated Parmesan + 1/2 cup regular breadcrumbs + 1 tsp garlic powder + 1 tsp Italian seasoning. Coat chicken, sear in 1 tbsp olive oil 2-3 min/side, then bake at 425°F for 8-12 min to 165°F. Optional sauce: 1/2 cup chicken broth + juice of 1/2 lemon + 1 tbsp butter.

**Serve with:** Roasted broccoli, green beans, Parmesan risotto, potatoes, simple salad

**Notes:** Crispy seared cutlets finished in oven; good high-protein dinner. With regular homemade breadcrumbs: ~680 calories and ~86g protein per large breast if eating one whole breast.
